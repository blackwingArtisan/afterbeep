# Demo script

Use this when preparing or recording the demo. Include it in a chat with `#demo`.

## The 60-second path
1. Landing page. Press **Play** on the machine: it rings, types Priya's burst water heater voicemail and shows the text she got back. Press **Bill**: the "dryer belt, fifteen" slip prints with an On hold stamp.
2. Press **Demo the whole story**. The call places itself, skips the ring and greeting, and ends on June Calloway's tape with the exact text in a phone and a TEXTED stamp.
3. **Open the text as June**, pick the first time, book.
4. **Back to Morgan: bill it by voice.** The slip script is already filled in. Line it up, use the usual $50.00, approve and send. The SENT stamp lands next to June's text.
5. The finale counts up what the story kept. Press **Pay as June**: PAID slams onto her invoice.
6. **Back to the machine: raise the dead.** Last week's five hang-ups wait as ghost cassettes. One press texts each a booking link, and they rise.
7. The inbox now reads Morgan's Tuesday: 2 booked, $557 of work, $35 caught, paid for itself 14 times.

## Before recording
- Press **Reset demo** first; the story uses a fresh 555-01xx caller ID each run.
- Run `npm run check` and `npm run test:e2e`; the whole story is covered by `tests/e2e/whole-story.spec.ts`.
- Lights on (daylight theme) reads better on video; the machine itself stays dark plastic.

## Words to use
Missed call, text back, booked, on hold, sent. Avoid "AI", "pipeline" and "LLM" on screen.
