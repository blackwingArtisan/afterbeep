# Social thread (draft)

1/ A one-truck plumber misses calls all day. Plain voicemail loses 69% of those callers. I brought the 1980s answering machine back from the dead to fix that. Meet AfterBeep, my #Kiroween entry. [GIF]

2/ Miss a call, and the caller gets a text with open times within seconds. Emergencies get flagged red and a promise of a call back. Everyone else books themselves.

3/ Then the part I'm proudest of. Say "dryer belt, fifteen" in the truck. Speech-to-text hears $15. AfterBeep checks your own past invoices, sees you always charge $50, and holds the bill. The machine that hears fifteen and knows you meant fifty.

4/ Code does the math, in whole cents. The server sends only the exact total you approved. No model ever adds anything up.

5/ Callers who hang up without a word go to the graveyard. One press raises the dead: each gets one text with a booking link.

6/ Built with @kirodotdev: steering that loads money rules with money code, 101 EARS criteria that fail CI without a test, and a hook that restates the money rules on every money change. $39 a month, about 7 cents a call on AWS. [link]
